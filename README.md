# Pharmacovigilance Internship – Task 2

## ADR Reporting & Documentation

### Objective

To understand and demonstrate the process of documenting and reporting an Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) using a structured pharmacovigilance reporting format.

### Case Summary

A 32-year-old male patient weighing 65 kg was diagnosed with acute bacterial sinusitis and was treated with amoxicillin 500 mg orally three times daily.

The patient developed generalized itching and skin rashes over the chest on 03/03/2026. Amoxicillin was stopped, following which the reaction abated. The patient recovered by 06/03/2026.

### Suspected Medicine

- **Medicine:** Amoxicillin
- **Dose:** 500 mg
- **Route:** Oral
- **Frequency:** Three times daily (TID)
- **Start Date:** 01/03/2026
- **Stop Date:** 03/03/2026
- **Indication:** Acute bacterial sinusitis

### Adverse Reaction

- **Reaction:** Generalized itching and skin rashes over the chest
- **Onset:** 03/03/2026
- **Recovery:** 06/03/2026
- **Outcome:** Recovered
- **Dechallenge:** Positive
- **Rechallenge:** Not applicable / not reported

### Pharmacovigilance Assessment

The case was documented based on the information provided in the training scenario. Missing information was recorded as not provided rather than being assumed or invented.

- ADR identification
- ADR documentation
- Suspected drug information collection
- Adverse reaction documentation
- Dechallenge assessment
- Case outcome documentation
- Pharmacovigilance case reporting
